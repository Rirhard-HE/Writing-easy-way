# Retcon / Clarification Candidates — Pass 1

These are not necessarily errors. They are places where the current draft will become fragile if the universe expands to millions of characters.

## P0 — Define “information” in three different senses

Current prose uses “information” for:
1. ordinary communication/data transmission;
2. encoded memory/personality/AI state;
3. possible ontological substrate of reality.

If these remain internally undefined, later dialogue can accidentally turn a metaphor into a hard rule.

**Recommendation:** internally define `SIGNAL_INFORMATION`, `IDENTITY_INFORMATION`, and `ONTOLOGICAL_INFORMATION`. Characters may conflate them; Novel OS may not.

## P0 — Define erasure semantics

The Grey Ash anomaly removes physical traces, while some video/data/personality continuation survives.

Need author rules for:
- object state;
- local physical evidence;
- remote records;
- observer memory;
- causal consequences;
- copies/backups;
- protected/archived states;
- whether “erasure” propagates across references to the erased object.

Without this, any future mystery can be solved or broken arbitrarily by “information deletion”.

## P0 — Define continuity / duplicate / missing

The Echo Well flags `连续性`, `存在重复`, `存在缺失` and `连续性中断`.

Need hidden hard rules even if characters never learn them early:
- what is a continuity chain?
- what counts as duplicate?
- whether two copies can both be valid;
- whether continuity is causal, informational, temporal, identity-based or something else;
- why He Yun is anomalous.

## P0 — Echo Well: artifact or access point?

Current draft supports both:
- “human technology”/old-Federation-compatible interfaces;
- research speculation that Echo Well was not manufactured but discovered.

This can be excellent layered mystery **if intentional**.

Recommended architecture:
- underlying phenomenon/system: `UNRESOLVED` or secret author truth;
- old-Federation interface/engineering: human-made;
- modern Federation access layer: recovered/partially understood.

## P1 — “Information is light” should be epistemically scoped

Early technical dialogue describes information transmission in conventional terms. If L0 truth is “information is reality”, do not delete the line automatically.

Recommended tag: `L2_MODERN_FEDERATION_MODEL` or a statement about ordinary long-distance signal transmission, not ontology.

## P1 — The draft may reveal the core concept too directly

A character explicitly entertains the idea that “matter, consciousness, personality … may all just be information.”

If the final philosophical reveal is meant to be late, this currently functions as a near-answer rather than only foreshadowing.

Possible fixes:
- make it one minority research hypothesis among several;
- attach evidence that appears to contradict it;
- narrow the claim to relic systems rather than all reality;
- preserve the sentence but make the reader misunderstand what “information” actually means.

## P1 — Great Collapse relationship to ontology

Current history explains the consequences of the Collapse well, but its fundamental cause is still open.

Need author decision before long expansion:
- directly caused by information-layer failure?
- caused by war/technology that damaged information continuity?
- unrelated historical collapse that later exposes the deeper ontology?

This choice will affect almost every later historical faction and relic.

## P1 — Hyperspace/curvature hierarchy

The text uses hyperspace anchors, drifting hyperspace structures, curvature bubbles, quantum-array interfaces and reality-space movement.

These can coexist, but Novel OS needs a hierarchy:
- what is the normal FTL mechanism?
- what does “hyperspace” mean physically/in-universe?
- what is a navigation anchor stabilizing?
- are curvature descriptions literal, approximate, or pedagogical?
- does information ontology sit below all of these rather than replacing them?

## P1 — AI personhood / continuity

Athena is already thematically positioned near human identity questions.

Need hidden decision:
- does an AI have the same type of ontological continuity as a human?
- can an AI be copied/reinstantiated and remain “the same”?
- can the Echo Well archive AI personality?

If left undefined too long, later interactions with He Yun may force accidental canon.

## P2 — Political sovereignty

The Federation is called a shell dominated by military megacorporations, but a meaningful Federal Navy and regulations still operate.

Need a formal model of:
- federal institutions;
- corporate sovereign powers;
- naval command/funding;
- courts/jurisdictions;
- colony ownership;
- chartered fleets;
- frontier enforcement.

This is less urgent for the mystery, but critical for a multi-million-character political expansion.

## P2 — Time and scale anchors

The draft provides useful numbers (roughly three centuries since the Collapse, an important relic opened 214 years ago, He Yun absent roughly half a year), but no single master chronology yet.

Before continuing, build a zero-point calendar and convert relative references into timeline entries.
