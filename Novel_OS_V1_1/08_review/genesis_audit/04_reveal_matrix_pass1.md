| Topic | Author truth | Current draft exposure | Reader state | Recommended status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## High reveal-risk passages

### “Matter, consciousness, personality … may all just be information”
Risk: high if the intended late reveal is simply the same proposition.

Recommendation: make the late reveal **more precise and stranger** than the early hypothesis. The early line can remain if what characters mean by “information” is incomplete/wrong.

### Echo Well functional labels
`保存 / 校验 / 连续性维护` are strong clues and fit the project very well. Keep them, but avoid explaining their target ontology too soon.

### Erasure from reality
The visual effect is strong and coherent. The key is to keep unclear whether the phenomenon destroys matter, edits state, removes history, or changes continuity.
