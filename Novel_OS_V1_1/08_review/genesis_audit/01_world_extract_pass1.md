## 2. Civilization history

### Pre-Collapse human civilization
- Status: `TEXT_CONFIRMED` at civilization-belief level
- Humanity once maintained inter-galactic or inter-galaxy route networks, stable gates, long-distance communication, and a much larger integrated civilization.
- The old system linked the Milky Way, Andromeda and other regions according to surviving accounts.

### The Great Collapse
- Status: `TEXT_CONFIRMED_EVENT / CAUSE_UNRESOLVED`
- Major route/gate systems failed; Andromeda became isolated from Milky Way civilization.
- A long period of fragmentation, war, famine, militarization and technical loss followed.
- Draft phrasing describes roughly three centuries of post-Collapse disorder/history.
- Important future decision: whether the Collapse is directly tied to L0 information ontology or is a separate civilization-scale failure.

### Technological survival pattern
- Status: `TEXT_CONFIRMED_SOCIAL_HISTORY`
- Highly integrated core worlds were vulnerable because advanced infrastructure depended on civilization-scale supply/maintenance networks.
- Peripheral colonies retained more rugged, repairable, lower-complexity systems.
- This helps explain why military/industrial frontier organizations survived and accumulated power.

## 4. Relics and recovered science

### Relic dependence
- Status: `TEXT_CONFIRMED`
- Much of modern advanced technology is described as reverse-engineered or operationally recovered from relic systems.
- Modern society often knows how to use systems without fully understanding why they work.

### Echo Well
- Status: `TEXT_CONFIRMED_ENTITY / OBJECTIVE_NATURE_UNRESOLVED`
- Associated with memory/personality preservation and revival workflows.
- Modern institutions access only an interface/terminal layer.
- Its main body is associated with a permanently restricted relic world and large quantum-array structures.
- It presents itself/functionally behaves as a system for preservation, verification, and continuity maintenance.
- It preserves civilization information, personality echoes and “fire”.
- A research hypothesis says the Echo Well may not have been manufactured at all; old humanity may only have discovered/accessed it.
- **High-priority author decision:** distinguish the underlying phenomenon from old-Federation interface engineering.

### ORPHEUS EDUCATION
- Status: `TEXT_CONFIRMED`
- An old-era education company/site is presented as an important source of neural teaching/training technology.
- This establishes that world-changing relic technology may originate from mundane pre-Collapse civilian institutions.
