# Author Decisions Queue

These questions are intentionally queued instead of guessed. They do not need to be answered all at once.

## Ontology — highest priority

1. What exactly does “信息即现实” mean in your setting?
2. Is ontological information conserved?
3. Can information be absolutely deleted?
4. What remains after deletion: causal effects, memories, records, remote copies?
5. Is identity a continuity relation, a data pattern, or something else?
6. Why is He Yun marked as both duplicate and missing?
7. Is the current He Yun the original continuity, a copy, both, or a category the characters do not yet understand?

## Echo Well

8. Is the Echo Well fundamentally a machine, a natural/ontological structure, a civilization-scale system, or an interface?
9. What did Old Federation humans actually build versus merely access?
10. What does “archive” mean operationally?
11. What is “fire” in `文明信息 / 人格回声 / 火种`?

## Great Collapse

12. What caused it at L0?
13. Did old humanity know the information-level truth before the Collapse?
14. Was knowledge intentionally lost/erased, or merely destroyed through civilizational failure?

## FTL / physics

15. Is hyperspace a real separate domain or a human model for deeper structure?
16. Are curvature bubbles and hyperspace jumps the same system, layered systems, or different technologies?
17. How do information-layer anomalies affect navigation and causality?

## AI

18. Does Athena have continuity in the same sense as a human?
19. Can AI personality be archived/restored by the Echo Well?
20. Can AI become “duplicate/missing” too?

## Reveal pacing

21. When should the reader first know that information is not merely a metaphor but the substrate of reality?
22. Which character/institution first possesses a substantially correct model?
23. What wrong theories should remain believable before the reveal?
